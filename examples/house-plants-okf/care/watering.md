# Watering

Watering is a decision based on plant type, pot size, medium, drainage, light, temperature, humidity, and current moisture. Calendar watering fails because plants use water differently as conditions change.

## Scope

This overview defines watering as a care domain. Use a [soil moisture check](/care/soil-moisture-check.md "requires") to decide when to water, set a suitable [dry-down](/care/dry-down.md "uses") target, and adjust for changing [watering demand](/care/watering-demand.md "adjusted by"). Good watering also prevents [overwatering](/care/overwatering.md "prevents") and [underwatering](/care/underwatering.md "prevents") when the pot has a working [drainage hole](/care/drainage-hole.md "depends on").
