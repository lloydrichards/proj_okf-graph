# Orchid

Many houseplant orchids, especially _Phalaenopsis_, are epiphytes adapted to airy root zones rather than dense potting soil.

## Care Graph

- Light: [bright indirect light](/care/bright-indirect-light.md "requires"), sometimes improved with [artificial lighting](/care/artificial-lighting.md "uses").
- Medium: airy bark-based [growing medium](/care/growing-medium.md "requires").
- Water: soak and drain thoroughly through a working [drainage hole](/care/drainage-hole.md "requires"); avoid standing water and [root rot](/problems/root-rot.md "at risk of").
- Temperature: comfortable indoor [temperature range](/care/temperature-range.md "requires"), with species-specific needs.
- Safety: [pet toxicity](/safety/pet-toxicity.md "has safety consideration") may be lower for common orchids, but verify the species.
