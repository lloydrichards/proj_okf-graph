type: Guide
title: Restoring a batch
description: How an operator restores an archived settlement batch.
sources:
  - resource: ../operations.md
---

Select the archived batch, verify its checksum, and submit it to the standard posting endpoint. Reconcile its entries after the run. See [posting identity](posting-identity.md) for how repeats are handled and the [activity feed](activity-feed.md) for interpreting receipts.
