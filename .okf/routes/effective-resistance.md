# Effective resistance

Imagine every link is an equal resistor, and send current between the two pages it joins. Current can take every available route. If the direct link is the only route, its effective resistance is 1. More and shorter detours lower the value. This view treats links as two-way.

## Houseplants example

Monstera and Monstera Yellow Leaves measure 1 because their direct link is the only undirected route between them. Microclimate and Maidenhair Fern measure about 0.596, the highest resistance among the non-bridges. Remove that link and a detour remains.

For protection of a specific directed route, compare [Menger’s theorem](./mengers-theorem.md).
