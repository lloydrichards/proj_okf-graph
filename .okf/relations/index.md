# Relations and evidence

What pages share and which links support an answer.

## Concepts

- [Typed path queries](./typed-path-queries.md) — Finds paths by matching the labels on their successive authored links.
- [Formal Concept Analysis](./formal-concept-analysis.md) — Finds sets of pages closed under their shared attributes.
- [Provenance semirings](./provenance-semirings.md) — Records which links are needed together and which alternative routes support a result.
- [Weisfeiler–Lehman refinement](./weisfeiler-lehman.md) — Compares structural roles by repeatedly grouping pages according to their neighbors and link labels.
