# Formal Concept Analysis

Start with some pages and keep the attributes they all share. Then ask which other pages have all of those attributes. Formal Concept Analysis repeats this idea to build groups you can browse from broad to narrow. In the houseplants example, an attribute is an authored link from a plant page to a care page.

## Houseplants example

Choose Drainage Hole and Growing Medium to find Aloe Vera and Orchid. Their pages link to both with the label `requires`. This gives a reader a comparison worth making, even though the plants need different light. An absent link means the bundle has no matching record for that page. It may still have an unlinked mention or the requirement may hold outside the bundle.

Finding the common attributes and then all pages with those attributes forms a Galois connection. The experiment used 13 plants and nine care targets and found seven closed groups with members.
