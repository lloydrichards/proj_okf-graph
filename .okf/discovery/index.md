# Discovery and context

Where a simulated reader goes and what connects the pages they find.

## Concepts

- [Markov chains](./markov-chains.md) — Describes a process whose next move depends on its current state.
- [Steiner trees](./steiner-trees.md) — Connects selected pages through a small shared set of links, with extra pages allowed as junctions.
- [Personalized PageRank](./pagerank.md) — Ranks pages by how often a simulated reader visits them when restarting at a chosen concept.
- [Infomap](./infomap.md) — Finds groups where a simulated reader frequently revisits nearby pages.
- [Two-hop link prediction](./two-hop-link-prediction.md) — Uses authored two-step routes as evidence for possible direct links.
- [Maximum coverage](./maximum-coverage.md) — Selects a fixed number of starting pages that collectively reach the most distinct concepts.
