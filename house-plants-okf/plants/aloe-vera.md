# Aloe Vera

Aloe vera is a common succulent grown for its rosette form. Indoors it needs bright conditions and excellent drainage.

## Care Graph

- Light: high light, often near [direct sun](/care/direct-sun.md "requires").
- Water: deep but infrequent watering with an appropriate [dry-down](/care/dry-down.md "uses") target.
- Medium: fast-draining [growing medium](/care/growing-medium.md "requires") and a container with a [drainage hole](/care/drainage-hole.md "requires").
- Problem: wet conditions can cause [root rot](/problems/root-rot.md "at risk of").
- Safety: [pet toxicity](/safety/pet-toxicity.md "has safety concern") matters because ASPCA lists aloe as toxic to cats and dogs.
